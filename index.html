Before you join

Frequently asked questions.

Where are the venues?

The tour visits three exclusive private locations in Medellín. Venue identities and arrival instructions are shared privately with confirmed participants.

What happens during the five days?

Days 1–2 feature rooftop poker, sports and entertainment. Days 3–4 move to a private VIP penthouse. Day 5 concludes at a secret private poker club.

Can I attend without playing poker?

We expect some rooftop hospitality and sports experiences to be open to approved non-playing guests. Availability and admission terms will be announced for each tour.

Are hotel rooms and transportation included?

Preferred room rates and VIP transportation benefits are being negotiated. Nothing is included unless specifically confirmed in your booking.

What games and stakes will be offered?

Game formats, stakes, buy-ins and table availability will be communicated ahead of each event and depend on demand and applicable requirements.

Does requesting an invitation reserve a seat?

No. An inquiry is not a reservation or guarantee of entry. All participation is subject to confirmation, capacity, age restrictions and applicable law.
